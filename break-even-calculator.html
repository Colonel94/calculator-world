Break-Even Calculator

Find the minimum whole units needed to cover fixed and variable costs.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter fixed costs, selling price per unit, variable cost per unit.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Contribution = price − variable cost. Break-even units = round up(fixed costs ÷ contribution).

Worked example

Fixed costs of 1,000 and contribution of 10 per unit require 100 sales to break even.

Assumptions and interpretation

All units sell at the same price and have the same variable cost. Price must exceed variable cost. Capacity limits and step changes in costs are not modeled.

Common question

Why round up?

A fraction of a sale cannot cover the remaining cost when you sell whole units.
