How to use this calculator

  1. Enter number.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

The principal square root is the nonnegative number r satisfying r × r = value.

Worked example

The principal square root of 144 is 12 because 12 × 12 = 144.

Assumptions and interpretation

Negative input requires complex numbers and is not supported here. A square-root result is distinct from the two solutions of x² = value.

Common question

Is −12 also a square root of 144?

It squares to 144, but the square-root symbol refers to the principal, nonnegative root.
