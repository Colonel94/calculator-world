Convert US or imperial miles per gallon to litres per 100 kilometres.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter miles per gallon, gallon definition.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

L/100 km = 100 × litres per gallon ÷ (MPG × 1.609344). US gallon = 3.785411784 L; imperial gallon = 4.54609 L.

Worked example

30 US mpg is about 7.840 L/100 km. The same numerical imperial mpg is about 9.416 L/100 km.

Assumptions and interpretation

Choose the gallon definition used by your source. US and UK fuel-economy figures are not directly interchangeable.

Common question

Why are imperial MPG figures higher for the same car?

An imperial gallon contains more fuel than a US gallon, so the vehicle travels more miles per gallon.
