Annualized Return Calculator

Convert growth between a beginning and ending value into a compound annual return.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter beginning value, ending value, term (years).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Annualized return (%) = ((ending value ÷ beginning value)^(1 ÷ years) − 1) × 100.

Worked example

Growth from 100 to 121 over 2 years is a compound annual return of 10%.

Assumptions and interpretation

Assumes no intermediate deposits or withdrawals. This is the constant yearly rate matching the endpoints, not the arithmetic average of annual returns.

Common question

Can the return be negative?

Yes. A lower ending value produces a negative rate; a total loss produces −100%.
