Paint Calculator

Estimate litres of paint for a stated area, coat count and manufacturer coverage.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter paintable area (m²), coverage per coat (m²/l), number of coats.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Paint litres = paintable area × number of coats ÷ coverage per litre per coat.

Worked example

40 m² with two coats at 10 m²/L requires 8 litres before extra allowance.

Assumptions and interpretation

Subtract unpainted openings from area. Coverage depends on the surface, product and method; primer is separate unless included in your figures.

Common question

Should I include windows and doors?

Exclude openings you will not paint. Include doors separately if they will receive the same paint.
