How to use this calculator

  1. Enter base (units), perpendicular height (units).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Triangle area = base × perpendicular height ÷ 2.

Worked example

A base of 10 and perpendicular height of 6 give an area of 30 square units.

Assumptions and interpretation

Height must be measured perpendicular to the chosen base, not along a sloping side. Both lengths must use the same unit.

Common question

Can the height fall outside the triangle?

Yes. For an obtuse triangle, the perpendicular height can meet an extension of the base.
