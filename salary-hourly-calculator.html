How to use this calculator

  1. Enter hourly pay, paid hours per week, paid weeks per year.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Annual pay = hourly pay × weekly hours × paid weeks. Average monthly pay = annual pay ÷ 12.

Worked example

25 per hour for 40 hours over 52 paid weeks is 52,000 per year, or 4,333.33 per average month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Use paid weeks, including paid leave where applicable. These are gross amounts before deductions and do not apply statutory overtime rules. Any currency, including AED, works if used consistently.

Common question

Why is monthly pay not weekly pay times four?

A year has more than 48 weeks. Dividing the annual amount by 12 gives a consistent monthly average.
