How to use this calculator

  1. Enter weight (kg), height (cm).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

BMI = weight in kilograms ÷ (height in metres)².

Worked example

At 70 kg and 175 cm, BMI = 70 ÷ 1.75² = 22.86 kg/m².

Assumptions and interpretation

For adults 20 and older. BMI is a screening measure, not a diagnosis or a direct measure of body fat. It does not account for muscle mass, pregnancy or individual medical circumstances.

Common question

Can I use this for a child?

No. Children and teens require age- and sex-specific interpretation. This tool does not provide that assessment.
