Loan Calculator

Estimate fixed monthly payments, total repayment and interest on an amortizing loan.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter loan amount, annual interest rate (%), term (years).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Payment = P × r ÷ (1 − (1 + r)⁻ⁿ), where r is annual interest ÷ 1,200 and n is years × 12. At zero interest, payment = P ÷ n.

Worked example

A 10,000 loan over 5 years at 5% has a monthly payment of about 188.71 and total interest of 1,322.74.

Assumptions and interpretation

Monthly payments are made at the end of each month. The rate stays fixed; fees, taxes and insurance are excluded. Use a term that gives a whole number of months.

Common question

Is the interest rate the same as APR?

Not necessarily. APR can incorporate fees. Enter the nominal annual interest rate quoted for the loan.
