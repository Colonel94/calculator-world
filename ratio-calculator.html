Ratio Simplifier

Reduce two whole-number quantities to their simplest ratio.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter first quantity, second quantity.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Divide both quantities by their greatest common divisor.

Worked example

24:36 reduces to 2:3 because both sides divide by 12.

Assumptions and interpretation

Use nonnegative whole numbers. Both sides cannot be zero. Decimal ratios can be multiplied by a power of ten before entry.

Common question

Can one side be zero?

Yes. 0:5 reduces to 0:1, but 0:0 has no meaningful simplified ratio.
