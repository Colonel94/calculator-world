Monthly Appliance Cost Calculator

Budget appliance energy and cost using daily runtime and billing-period length.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter average operating power (w), hours per day, days in billing period, price per kwh.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Period energy = watts ÷ 1,000 × daily hours × days. Cost = energy × unit price.

Worked example

150 W for 8 hours daily over 30 days uses 36 kWh and costs 10.80 at 0.30/kWh.

Assumptions and interpretation

The billing period is editable. Nameplate maximum power may overstate consumption for a cycling appliance; measured average power is preferable.

Common question

Does this include fixed utility charges?

No. It estimates the variable cost attributable to this appliance.
