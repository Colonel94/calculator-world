Wallpaper Calculator

Estimate wallpaper rolls using wall perimeter, height and pattern allowance.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter total wall perimeter (m), wall height (m), roll width (m), roll length (m), extra per strip for trimming/pattern (m).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Strip length = wall height + extra. Strips per roll = floor(roll length ÷ strip length). Rolls = round up(round up(perimeter ÷ roll width) ÷ strips per roll).

Worked example

An 18 m perimeter, 2.5 m height, 0.53 × 10 m rolls and 0.1 m extra gives 34 strips, three per roll, so 12 rolls.

Assumptions and interpretation

This conservatively includes openings in the perimeter and assumes full-height strips. Enter enough extra length for trimming and pattern repeat matching.

Common question

Why not divide wall area by roll area?

Roll offcuts may be too short for a full-height strip. Counting usable strips better reflects this waste.
