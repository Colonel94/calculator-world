Electricity Cost Calculator

Calculate appliance electricity consumption and cost for a total number of operating hours.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter power (watts), total operating hours, electricity price per kwh.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Energy (kWh) = watts ÷ 1,000 × hours. Cost = energy × tariff per kWh.

Worked example

A 1,000 W appliance running for 5 hours uses 5 kWh and costs 1.50 at 0.30/kWh.

Assumptions and interpretation

Power is assumed constant while operating. Use average measured power for cycling equipment. Standing charges, taxes and tiered tariffs are not added.

Common question

Should I enter hours per day?

This tool expects total hours for the period. Multiply hours per day by days first, or use Monthly Appliance Cost.
