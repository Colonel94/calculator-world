Commission Calculator

Estimate percentage commission plus an optional fixed base payment.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter eligible sales, commission rate (%), base pay for this period.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Commission = eligible sales × rate ÷ 100. Total pay = commission + base pay.

Worked example

10,000 eligible sales at 5% earns 500 commission; with 1,000 base pay the total is 1,500.

Assumptions and interpretation

This models one flat rate. Returns, quotas, tiered rates and tax withholding are excluded. Use the same period for sales and base pay.

Common question

Can I use this for a tiered plan?

Calculate each tier separately and add the amounts. A flat rate across all sales may overstate or understate a tiered commission.
