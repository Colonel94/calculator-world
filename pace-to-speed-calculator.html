Pace to Speed Calculator

Convert a minutes-per-kilometre pace into kilometres per hour and miles per hour.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter pace (decimal minutes/km).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Speed in km/h = 60 ÷ pace in min/km. Speed in mph = km/h ÷ 1.609344.

Worked example

A 6 min/km pace is 10 km/h, or about 6.214 mph.

Assumptions and interpretation

Pace must be greater than zero. This is a unit conversion and does not estimate exercise effort or calories.

Common question

Why does a lower pace number mean faster movement?

It means fewer minutes are needed to cover each kilometre.
