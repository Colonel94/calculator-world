Raise a number to an integer or fractional power in the real-number system.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter base, exponent.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Result = base raised to the exponent. A negative exponent gives the reciprocal of the corresponding positive power.

Worked example

2 raised to 10 is 1,024; 2 raised to −3 is 0.125.

Assumptions and interpretation

Zero to a nonpositive power is excluded. Negative bases require integer exponents here. Results outside reliable finite output are rejected.

Common question

Can I calculate a root?

For a positive base, a power of 0.5 gives the square root.
