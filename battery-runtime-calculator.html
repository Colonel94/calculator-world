How to use this calculator

  1. Enter battery capacity (ah), nominal voltage (v), load power (w), usable capacity (%), system efficiency (%).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Usable energy (Wh) = Ah × volts × usable fraction × efficiency. Runtime (h) = usable energy ÷ load watts.

Worked example

100 Ah at 12 V with 80% usable capacity and 90% efficiency supplies about 864 Wh, or 8.64 hours at 100 W.

Assumptions and interpretation

This is an energy-budget approximation. Temperature, battery age, discharge rate, inverter idle draw and voltage cutoff can shorten runtime.

Common question

Why is this only an estimate?

Rated capacity is measured under specific conditions, and real capacity and conversion losses vary with the system.
