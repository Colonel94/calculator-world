Age Calculator

Calculate completed years of age on a date you choose.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter date of birth, age on date.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Completed years = reference year − birth year, minus one if the birthday has not yet occurred in the reference year.

Worked example

Someone born on June 15, 1990 is 36 on September 19, 2026, and 35 on June 14, 2026.

Assumptions and interpretation

Future births relative to the reference date are rejected. A February 29 birthday is treated as occurring on March 1 in non-leap years; legal conventions can differ.

Common question

Is this a legal age determination?

No. This uses a stated calendar convention; legal rules for leap-day birthdays or eligibility may differ.
