Net Profit Calculator

Calculate profit after the business costs and tax expense you enter.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter revenue, cost of goods sold, other operating and financing expenses, tax expense.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Net profit = revenue − cost of goods sold − other expenses − tax expense.

Worked example

10,000 revenue minus 4,000 goods, 3,000 other expenses and 500 tax leaves 2,500 net profit.

Assumptions and interpretation

Enter expenses for the same reporting period. Tax is an input, not a calculation of legal liability. Avoid counting the same expense twice.

Common question

Is a negative result possible?

Yes. A negative result is a net loss for the period based on the costs you entered.
