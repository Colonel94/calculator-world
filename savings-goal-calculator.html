Savings Goal Calculator

Find the monthly saving needed to reach a target balance by your chosen deadline.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter target balance, already saved, annual interest rate (%), months to save.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Monthly saving = max(0, (target − P(1+r)ⁿ) ÷ annuity factor). Annuity factor = ((1+r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r, or n at zero interest.

Worked example

To grow 2,000 to 8,000 over 24 months at zero interest, save 250 per month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Deposits are made at month end and interest compounds monthly. A zero result means the starting balance alone reaches the target under the entered assumptions.

Common question

What if I already have enough saved?

The required monthly contribution is zero. This tool does not recommend withdrawing the surplus.
