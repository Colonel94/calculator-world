Sphere Calculator

Calculate volume and surface area of a sphere from its radius.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter radius (units).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Volume = 4πr³ ÷ 3. Surface area = 4πr².

Worked example

A radius of 3 gives both a volume of about 113.10 cubic units and an area of 113.10 square units.

Assumptions and interpretation

This models a complete sphere, not a hemisphere or hollow shell. For a hollow object calculate the inner and outer volumes separately.

Common question

What if I measured the diameter?

Halve the diameter before entering the radius.
