Unit Price Calculator

Compare two packages by cost per matching unit of quantity.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter package a price, package a quantity, package b price, package b quantity.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Unit price = package price ÷ quantity. Compare the two unit prices in the same measurement unit.

Worked example

A 750 g package costing 6 costs 0.008 per gram; a 1,000 g package costing 9 costs 0.009 per gram. Package A is cheaper per gram.

Assumptions and interpretation

Both quantities must use the same unit, such as grams, litres or pieces. Shipping and quality differences are outside the comparison.

Common question

Can I compare grams with kilograms directly?

Convert both quantities to the same unit first; otherwise the unit-price comparison will be wrong.
