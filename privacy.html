Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 19, 2026.

Calculator inputs

Calculations run in your browser. This site does not send calculator inputs to a server, store them in cookies or browser storage, or require an account. Values are not intentionally saved after you leave a page.

Hosting and external links

GitHub Pages hosts this website. Requests to the host necessarily include technical connection data, such as an IP address and requested URL. See GitHub’s privacy statement. Following an external link takes you to a service with its own privacy practices.

Advertising and analytics

Google AdSense and Google Analytics are not currently enabled. No advertising or analytics cookies are intentionally set by this website. If these services are activated, this policy will first be updated to identify the providers, data uses, cookies, user choices and any applicable consent mechanism. Enabling future services may allow Google and its partners to process data for advertising or measurement.

Contact and changes

Reports made through GitHub are handled by GitHub and may be public. Do not post private financial or health information. Visit Contact for the reporting route. This policy will be revised when site practices change.
