Hours Worked Calculator

Calculate paid work hours after an unpaid break for a same-day or overnight shift.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter shift start, shift end, unpaid break (minutes), end day.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Paid minutes = end − start + overnight adjustment − unpaid break. Paid hours = paid minutes ÷ 60.

Worked example

09:00 to 17:30 with a 30-minute unpaid break is 8 paid hours.

Assumptions and interpretation

Break time cannot exceed the shift. This does not apply overtime, rounding policies or labor-law rules; it only calculates the entered schedule.

Common question

Does the result include paid breaks?

Paid breaks are already part of the shift; enter only the time that should be deducted.
