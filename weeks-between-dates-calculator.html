Weeks Between Dates Calculator

Express a calendar interval as complete weeks and remaining days.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter start date, end date.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Complete weeks = floor(elapsed days ÷ 7). Remaining days = elapsed days modulo 7.

Worked example

A 31-day interval is 4 complete weeks and 3 remaining days.

Assumptions and interpretation

Uses the absolute elapsed interval and excludes the starting date. A week is always seven days; this does not count ISO calendar week numbers.

Common question

Are these calendar week numbers?

No. This divides elapsed days into seven-day groups regardless of the weekday on which you start.
