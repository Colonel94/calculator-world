Rent Payment Calculator

Convert an annual rental amount into equal instalments and a monthly average.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter annual rent, equal payments per year.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Each instalment = annual rent ÷ number of payments. Monthly average = annual rent ÷ 12.

Worked example

Annual rent of 60,000 paid in four equal instalments is 15,000 per payment and averages 5,000 per month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Useful for comparing annual rent paid by one or several cheques, including AED budgets. Deposits, commissions, utilities and registration fees are excluded unless added separately.

Common question

Does changing the payment count change annual cost?

Not here. If a landlord quotes a different annual price for another schedule, enter that price separately.
