# Formula sources and conventions

Reviewed September 19, 2026. Links support definitions and assumptions; explanations and examples are original.

- [NIST conversion factors](https://www.nist.gov/pml/special-publication-811/nist-guide-si-appendix-b-conversion-factors): international foot 0.3048 m, inch 0.0254 m, mile 1609.344 m, pound 0.45359237 kg, US liquid gallon 3.785411784 L, imperial gallon 4.54609 L and atmosphere 101325 Pa. Areas/volumes use the appropriate powers. PSI is represented to finite precision.
- [NIST binary prefixes](https://physics.nist.gov/cuu/Units/binary.html): powers of 1,000 versus powers of 1,024; bits versus bytes.
- [CFPB mortgage explanation](https://www.consumerfinance.gov/ask-cfpb/how-do-mortgage-lenders-calculate-monthly-payments-en-1965/): fixed amortization and the 100,000 / 4% / 30-year reference, approximately 477 monthly; unrounded test value 477.4152954654538.
- [CDC adult BMI](https://www.cdc.gov/bmi/adult-calculator/index.html): adult scope and screening limitations. The tool gives an index, not diagnosis or weight targets.
- [UAE FTA VAT FAQ](https://tax.gov.ae/en/faq.aspx?keyword=Does+VAT+apply+to+all+goods+and+services%3F): standard rate and exceptions. Rate remains editable; transaction classification is not automated.
- [Google robots rules](https://developers.google.com/search/docs/crawling-indexing/robots/robots_txt): effective host-root location.
- [AdSense privacy requirements](https://support.google.com/adsense/answer/1348695): disclosures needed when services are activated.

Dates use the proleptic Gregorian calendar and UTC midnight. Month and leap-day conventions are explicit. Business days exclude only the chosen weekend, not holidays. Construction tools estimate quantities rather than structural adequacy; density and waste are editable. Finance uses fixed-rate scenarios with no return forecast. Monetary calculations accept any consistent currency; no live foreign exchange is included.
