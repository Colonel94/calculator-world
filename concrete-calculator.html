Concrete Slab Calculator

Estimate concrete volume for a rectangular slab with an adjustable ordering allowance.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter length (m), width (m), thickness (cm), extra allowance (%).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Net volume = length × width × thickness ÷ 100. Order volume = net volume × (1 + allowance ÷ 100).

Worked example

A 5 m by 4 m slab, 10 cm thick, needs 2 m³ net or 2.2 m³ with 10% extra.

Assumptions and interpretation

The slab is uniform and rectangular. Allowance covers the extra quantity you choose; it does not specify structural design, reinforcement or a suitable slab thickness.

Common question

Does this decide the correct slab thickness?

No. Enter a thickness specified for your project; the calculator only estimates quantity.
