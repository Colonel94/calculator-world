Tile Calculator

Estimate whole tiles needed from area, tile dimensions and a waste allowance.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter area to tile (m²), tile length (cm), tile width (cm), waste allowance (%).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Tile area = length × width ÷ 10,000. Tiles = round up(area × (1 + waste ÷ 100) ÷ tile area).

Worked example

20 m² using 30 × 30 cm tiles with 10% waste requires 245 whole tiles.

Assumptions and interpretation

Uses tile face dimensions and ignores grout joints. Cuts, pattern matching, breakage and pack sizes can increase the purchase quantity.

Common question

Why does the estimate ignore grout?

Grout can slightly reduce the tile count, but layout and cuts usually matter more. Confirm against a scaled layout for final ordering.
