Present Value Calculator

Discount a future lump sum into its equivalent value today.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter future amount, annual interest rate (%), years until receipt.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Present value = future amount ÷ (1 + annual discount rate ÷ 100)^years.

Worked example

1,100 received in one year discounted at 10% has a present value of 1,000.

Assumptions and interpretation

The discount rate is your assumed annual effective return or opportunity cost. The calculation models a single future payment.

Common question

Is the discount rate an inflation forecast?

It can reflect inflation or an opportunity cost, but you must choose and enter the assumption yourself.
