Debt Payoff Calculator

Estimate months and interest needed to repay a debt with a fixed monthly payment.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter current balance, annual interest rate (%), monthly payment.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Each month: interest = balance × annual rate ÷ 1,200; new balance = balance + interest − payment. The final payment is reduced to the amount due.

Worked example

A balance of 1,000 paid at 100 per month with zero interest takes 10 months.

Assumptions and interpretation

The rate and payment remain fixed with no new borrowing or fees. Actual credit cards may accrue interest daily. Payments that cannot cover interest are rejected.

Common question

Why is my payment rejected?

A payment at or below monthly interest does not reduce the starting balance, so it cannot pay off the debt.
