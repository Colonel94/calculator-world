Revenue Growth Calculator

Compare business revenue across two comparable reporting periods.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter earlier period revenue, later period revenue.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Revenue growth (%) = (later revenue − earlier revenue) ÷ earlier revenue × 100.

Worked example

Revenue rising from 10,000 to 12,500 is an increase of 2,500, or 25%.

Assumptions and interpretation

Compare periods of equal length and consistent accounting treatment. The earlier revenue must be positive; this is not a profit-growth calculation.

Common question

Can I compare a month with a full year?

The arithmetic works but the percentage is misleading. Compare equal-length periods such as the same month in successive years.
