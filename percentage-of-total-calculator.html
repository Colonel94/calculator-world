Percentage of Total Calculator

Find what percentage one quantity represents of a total.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter part, total.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Percentage = part ÷ total × 100.

Worked example

25 out of 200 represents 12.5% of the total.

Assumptions and interpretation

The total cannot be zero. Values over 100% are possible if the part exceeds a positive total; signed values are accepted for mathematical use.

Common question

Can the result exceed 100%?

Yes. For example, 250 relative to a total of 200 is 125%.
