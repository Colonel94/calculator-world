Average Calculator

Calculate the arithmetic mean, sum and count of a list of numbers.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter numbers (comma or space separated).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Mean = sum of values ÷ number of values.

Worked example

For 10, 20, 30 and 40, the sum is 100 and the arithmetic mean is 25.

Assumptions and interpretation

All values have equal weight. Use commas or spaces to separate numbers and a dot as the decimal separator; commas are not thousands separators.

Common question

Can I include negative numbers?

Yes. For example, −10 and 10 have an average of zero.
