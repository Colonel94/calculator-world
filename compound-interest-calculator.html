Compound Interest Calculator

Calculate balance growth with annual, monthly or daily compounding.

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter starting amount, annual interest rate (%), years, compounding frequency.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Future balance = P × (1 + annual rate ÷ 100 ÷ periods)^(periods × years).

Worked example

1,000 at 5% compounded annually for 2 years becomes 1,102.50, a gain of 102.50.

Assumptions and interpretation

The interest rate is nominal and fixed. There are no deposits, withdrawals, taxes or fees. Daily compounding uses 365 periods per year.

Common question

Why does frequency change the result?

More frequent compounding lets interest start earning interest earlier when the nominal rate is unchanged.
