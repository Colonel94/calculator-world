How to use this calculator

  1. Enter cost, selling price.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Profit = price − cost. Margin = profit ÷ price × 100. Markup = profit ÷ cost × 100.

Worked example

Cost of 60 and a price of 100 give 40 profit, 40% margin and 66.67% markup.

Assumptions and interpretation

Margin and markup have different denominators. A zero denominator makes its percentage undefined; it is not reported as zero. This excludes overhead and tax.

Common question

Why are margin and markup different?

Margin expresses profit as a share of sales. Markup expresses it as a share of the cost you paid.
