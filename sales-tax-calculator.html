Sales Tax Calculator

Calculate sales tax and checkout total using an editable combined tax rate.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter taxable subtotal, combined sales tax rate (%).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Sales tax = taxable subtotal × rate ÷ 100. Total = subtotal + tax.

Worked example

A taxable subtotal of 100 at 8% sales tax adds 8, for a total of 108.

Assumptions and interpretation

Enter the applicable combined rate yourself. Exempt items, tax holidays, shipping rules and jurisdiction-specific rounding are not determined here.

Common question

Does this find my local tax rate?

No. Use a rate from the relevant tax authority and enter only the taxable subtotal.
