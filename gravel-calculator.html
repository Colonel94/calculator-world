Gravel Calculator

Estimate loose gravel volume and mass using your supplier’s bulk density.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter coverage area (m²), depth (cm), bulk density (kg/m³).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Volume = area × depth ÷ 100. Mass = volume × bulk density.

Worked example

20 m² at 5 cm depth is 1 m³. At an assumed 1,600 kg/m³ that is 1,600 kg or 1.6 tonnes.

Assumptions and interpretation

Density varies by stone, moisture and compaction. The default is an illustrative assumption; replace it with supplier data. Compaction allowance is not automatically added.

Common question

Is the default density exact?

No. It is only an example; use the density of the product you are ordering.
