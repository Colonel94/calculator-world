Dates & Time

Add or Subtract Days Calculator

Find the date a signed number of calendar days before or after a starting date.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter starting date, days to add (negative to subtract).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Result date = starting UTC-midnight date + signed day count × 86,400,000 milliseconds.

Worked example

Adding 30 days to January 1, 2026 gives January 31, 2026. Subtracting 1 gives December 31, 2025.

Assumptions and interpretation

All calendar days count, including weekends and holidays. Dates must remain within years 0001 through 9999.

Common question

Are weekends skipped?

No. Use a business-day calculation when you need to exclude weekend days.
