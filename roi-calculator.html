ROI Calculator

Calculate the percentage return and gain or loss on an investment.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter amount invested, final value including distributions.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

ROI (%) = (final value − initial investment) ÷ initial investment × 100.

Worked example

An investment costing 1,000 with a final value of 1,200 returns 20%, or 200.

Assumptions and interpretation

Include cash distributions in final value and costs in the initial amount. This is a total-period return, not an annualized return.

Common question

Can I compare investments held for different lengths of time?

Total ROI ignores time. Compare equal periods or use annualized return for a time-adjusted comparison.
