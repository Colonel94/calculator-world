Mortgage Calculator

Estimate mortgage principal and interest plus editable monthly housing costs.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter mortgage principal, annual interest rate (%), term (years), monthly taxes, insurance and other costs.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Monthly housing estimate = amortizing principal-and-interest payment + monthly extra costs.

Worked example

At 4% for 30 years, a 100,000 mortgage costs about 477.42 per month before extra costs.

Assumptions and interpretation

This is a fixed-rate loan estimate. Enter extra costs as a monthly total; down payments should already be deducted from principal. No jurisdiction-specific lending rules are assumed.

Common question

Does this include property taxes?

Only if you include them in monthly extra costs. Convert annual bills to monthly amounts first.
