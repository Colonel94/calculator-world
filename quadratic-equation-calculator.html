How to use this calculator

  1. Enter coefficient a, coefficient b, coefficient c.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Discriminant D = b² − 4ac. Roots = (−b ± √D) ÷ 2a.

Worked example

x² − 3x + 2 = 0 has discriminant 1 and roots 1 and 2.

Assumptions and interpretation

Coefficient a must be nonzero. Negative discriminants have no real roots. A numerically stable equivalent formula is used to reduce cancellation for unequal roots.

Common question

What does a zero discriminant mean?

Both real roots coincide at −b/(2a), often called a repeated root.
