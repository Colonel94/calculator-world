Successive Discounts Calculator

Combine two sequential discounts without incorrectly adding their percentages.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter original price, first discount (%), second discount (%).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Final price = original × (1 − first ÷ 100) × (1 − second ÷ 100).

Worked example

20% off then another 10% off a price of 100 gives 72, equivalent to 28% off.

Assumptions and interpretation

The second discount applies to the already discounted price. This assumes both promotions can be combined and excludes tax.

Common question

Why is 20% plus 10% not 30% off?

The second 10% is taken from 80, so it saves 8 rather than 10.
