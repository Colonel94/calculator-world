Cylinder Volume Calculator

Find the volume and total surface area of a closed circular cylinder.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter radius (units), height (units).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Volume = πr²h. Closed surface area = 2πr(r + h).

Worked example

Radius 3 and height 10 give a volume of about 282.74 cubic units.

Assumptions and interpretation

The cylinder has a constant circular cross-section. Surface area includes both end circles and the curved wall. Use consistent units.

Common question

Does surface area include the ends?

Yes, both circular ends are included. An open container needs a different surface-area total.
