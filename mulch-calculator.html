Mulch Calculator

Estimate mulch volume and bag count from coverage area, depth and bag size.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter coverage area (m²), depth (cm), bag volume (l).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Volume in litres = area in m² × depth in cm × 10. Bags = round up(litres ÷ bag litres).

Worked example

20 m² covered to 5 cm needs 1,000 L of mulch, or twenty 50 L bags.

Assumptions and interpretation

Assumes a uniform layer and uses the stated loose bag volume. Settling, uneven ground and topping up an existing layer can change requirements.

Common question

Can I calculate only a top-up layer?

Yes. Enter the additional depth you want to add, not the total final depth.
