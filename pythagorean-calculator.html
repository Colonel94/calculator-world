Pythagorean Theorem Calculator

Find the hypotenuse of a right triangle from its two perpendicular sides.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter first perpendicular side, second perpendicular side.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Hypotenuse c = √(a² + b²).

Worked example

Perpendicular sides of 3 and 4 give a hypotenuse of 5.

Assumptions and interpretation

This applies only to a right triangle. Enter the two sides adjacent to the right angle in the same length unit.

Common question

Does this work for every triangle?

No. The angle between the two entered sides must be exactly 90 degrees.
