How to use this calculator

  1. Enter value, from unit, to unit.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Converted value = input × source-unit factor ÷ destination-unit factor. Factors use a shared base unit.

Worked example

1 US customary cup equals 16 US tablespoons or about 236.588 mL.

Assumptions and interpretation

These are volume measurements, not ingredient weights. US customary cups differ from 240 mL nutrition-label cups and 250 mL metric cups. Australian tablespoons may use 20 mL.

Common question

Why must the unit definition match?

Similar names can represent different sizes. Choose the exact unit named on your source measurement.
