Annual Fuel Cost Calculator

Budget yearly and average monthly fuel costs from annual mileage.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter annual distance (km), consumption (l/100 km), fuel price per litre.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Annual fuel = annual kilometres × L/100 km ÷ 100. Annual cost = fuel × price. Monthly average = annual cost ÷ 12.

Worked example

15,000 km at 7 L/100 km and 3 per litre costs 3,150 annually, or 262.50 per average month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Uses a constant fuel price and consumption rate. Seasonal changes, idling and unusually heavy traffic can change actual use.

Common question

Can I use an average fuel price?

Yes. A weighted average across your expected purchases can make a more useful budget than a single-day price.
