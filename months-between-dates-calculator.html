Months Between Dates Calculator

Count complete calendar months using the starting day as the monthly anniversary.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter start date, end date.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Months = (end year − start year) × 12 + end month − start month; subtract one if the end day is earlier than the start day.

Worked example

January 15 to April 14 is two complete months; through April 15 it is three.

Assumptions and interpretation

End must not precede start. The same day-of-month is required for a complete month; January 31 to February 28 is zero complete months under this convention.

Common question

Is every month treated as 30 days?

No. This compares calendar fields and does not convert days using an average month length.
