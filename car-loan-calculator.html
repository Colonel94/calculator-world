Car Loan Calculator

Estimate vehicle payments after a deposit and trade-in, with optional financed fees.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter vehicle price, deposit, trade-in equity, financed fees, annual interest rate (%), term (months).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Amount financed = price + fees − deposit − trade-in equity. Apply the monthly amortizing payment formula to that amount.

Worked example

A 25,000 vehicle with a 5,000 deposit and zero interest over 60 months costs 333.33 per month.

Assumptions and interpretation

Trade-in equity means the value remaining after any old loan is settled. Enter negative equity as financed fees. Insurance and running costs are excluded.

Common question

Should I enter the full trade-in value?

Subtract any outstanding loan first, so you enter only the equity available for the new purchase.
