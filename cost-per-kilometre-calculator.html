Cost per Kilometre Calculator

Estimate total driving cost per kilometre from distance and annual ownership costs.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter annual distance (km), annual fuel cost, other annual costs.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Cost per kilometre = (annual fuel cost + other annual costs) ÷ annual distance.

Worked example

3,000 fuel plus 2,000 other costs across 15,000 km gives about 0.3333 per km.

Assumptions and interpretation

Include insurance, maintenance, depreciation and fees in other costs if you want a broader ownership estimate. Avoid double-counting loan principal and depreciation.

Common question

Does the result include depreciation?

Only if you include an annual depreciation estimate in other costs.
