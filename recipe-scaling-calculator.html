Recipe Scaling Calculator

Scale an ingredient amount when changing the number of recipe servings.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter original servings, target servings, original ingredient quantity.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Scale factor = target servings ÷ original servings. New ingredient quantity = original quantity × scale factor.

Worked example

Scaling a 4-serving recipe to 6 servings multiplies ingredients by 1.5, so 200 g becomes 300 g.

Assumptions and interpretation

The output uses the same unit as the entered ingredient amount. Cooking time, pan size, seasonings and leavening may not scale linearly.

Common question

Should I multiply cooking time by the same factor?

No. Cooking time depends on thickness, vessel and heating conditions, so follow suitable cooking guidance.
