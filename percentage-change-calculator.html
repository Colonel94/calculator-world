Percentage Change Calculator

Measure a relative increase or decrease between an old and new value.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter old value, new value.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Change (%) = (new − old) ÷ absolute value of old × 100.

Worked example

A rise from 80 to 100 is a difference of 20 and a percentage increase of 25%.

Assumptions and interpretation

A zero starting value has no defined percentage change. For negative starting values we explicitly use the absolute denominator so a numerical increase stays positive.

Common question

Why does reversing the values change the percentage?

The base changes. Falling from 100 to 80 is 20%, while rising from 80 to 100 is 25%.
