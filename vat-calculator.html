VAT Calculator

Add VAT to a net price or extract it from a tax-inclusive total using your own rate.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter amount, vat rate (%), amount entered.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Gross = net × (1 + rate ÷ 100). Net = gross ÷ (1 + rate ÷ 100). VAT = gross − net.

Worked example

At 5%, a net amount of 100 becomes 105. Extracting VAT from 105 returns net 100 and VAT 5.

Assumptions and interpretation

The rate is editable and this tool does not decide whether a supply is taxable, exempt or zero-rated. For UAE examples, 5% is the standard rate according to the Federal Tax Authority; confirm treatment before invoicing.

Common question

Can I subtract 5% from an inclusive price?

No. Divide by 1.05 to remove 5% VAT. Subtracting 5% of the gross price removes too much.
