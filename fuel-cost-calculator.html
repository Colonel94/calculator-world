Fuel & Trip Cost Calculator

Estimate trip fuel use and cost from distance, consumption and price per litre.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter trip distance (km), consumption (l/100 km), fuel price per litre.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Litres used = distance × consumption ÷ 100. Cost = litres × price per litre.

Worked example

300 km at 7 L/100 km uses 21 litres. At 3 per litre the fuel costs 63.

Assumptions and interpretation

Enter the total trip distance, including the return journey if needed. Consumption and prices are your assumptions; tolls, parking and maintenance are excluded.

Common question

Are fuel prices updated automatically?

No. Enter the current price you expect to pay; any currency including AED can be used.
