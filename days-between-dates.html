Days Between Dates Calculator

Count elapsed calendar days between two dates without daylight-saving errors.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter start date, end date.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Elapsed days = absolute difference between UTC-midnight dates ÷ 86,400,000 milliseconds.

Worked example

January 1 to February 1, 2026 is 31 elapsed calendar days; January 1 to itself is zero.

Assumptions and interpretation

The start day is excluded. Both dates can be reversed without changing the absolute interval. These are calendar days, not elapsed local-clock hours.

Common question

How do I include both dates?

For an inclusive count of calendar dates, add one to the elapsed-day result.
