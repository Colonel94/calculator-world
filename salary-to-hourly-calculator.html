Salary to Hourly Calculator

Estimate the hourly equivalent of an annual salary using your paid work schedule.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter annual gross salary, paid hours per week, paid weeks per year.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Hourly equivalent = annual salary ÷ (hours per week × paid weeks per year).

Worked example

An annual salary of 52,000 over 40 hours and 52 weeks is equivalent to 25 per hour.

Assumptions and interpretation

This is an average gross equivalent, not an overtime entitlement or legal pay calculation. Use AED or another currency consistently.

Common question

Should unpaid leave count as paid weeks?

No. Reduce paid weeks to reflect the schedule you are comparing.
