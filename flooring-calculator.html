Flooring Calculator

Estimate flooring purchase area and material cost including waste.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter net floor area (m²), waste allowance (%), material price per m², coverage per pack (m²).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Required area = net area × (1 + waste ÷ 100). Packs = round up(required area ÷ pack coverage). Purchase cost = packs × pack coverage × price per m².

Worked example

20 m² plus 10% waste is 22 m². With 2 m² packs, buy 11 packs; at 25/m² the material cost is 550.

Assumptions and interpretation

Cost reflects whole packs, not just measured area. Labor, underlay, delivery and trim are excluded. Use the manufacturer’s pack coverage.

Common question

Why can purchased area exceed the waste-adjusted area?

Flooring is often sold in full packs, so the final pack is rounded up.
