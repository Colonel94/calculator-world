Loan Comparison Calculator

Compare payments and lifetime interest for two fixed-rate loan offers.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter amount borrowed, offer a annual rate (%), offer a months, offer b annual rate (%), offer b months.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Calculate each payment using the amortizing-loan formula; total interest = monthly payment × number of payments − principal.

Worked example

At zero interest, 12,000 over 12 months costs 1,000 monthly; over 24 months it costs 500 monthly. Both repay 12,000.

Assumptions and interpretation

Both offers finance the same amount and exclude fees. A lower monthly payment may cost more overall if the term is longer.

Common question

Which offer is better?

Compare both affordability and total cost. This mathematical comparison does not evaluate contract terms or your financial circumstances.
