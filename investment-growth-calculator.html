Investment Growth Calculator

Project a starting investment with regular end-of-month contributions.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter starting amount, monthly contribution, annual interest rate (%), years.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Future value = P(1 + r)ⁿ + D × ((1 + r)ⁿ − 1) ÷ r, with r = annual rate ÷ 1,200 and n = years × 12.

Worked example

Starting with 1,000 and adding 100 monthly for a year at zero growth gives 2,200.

Assumptions and interpretation

Contributions arrive at month end. Returns are a constant hypothetical rate, not a forecast. Fees, taxes and market fluctuations are excluded.

Common question

Are contributions invested at the start of the month?

No. This uses end-of-month contributions, so the final deposit earns no growth within the period.
