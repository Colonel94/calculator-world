Circle Area & Circumference Calculator

Calculate a circle’s area, circumference and diameter from its radius.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter radius (units).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Area = πr². Circumference = 2πr. Diameter = 2r.

Worked example

A radius of 5 gives an area of about 78.54 square units and circumference of 31.42 units.

Assumptions and interpretation

Use any consistent length unit. Area is reported in the corresponding squared unit; circumference and diameter use the original unit.

Common question

I have the diameter. What should I enter?

Divide the diameter by two to obtain the radius.
