Simple Interest Calculator

Find interest on principal when interest does not compound.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter starting amount, annual interest rate (%), years.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Interest = principal × annual rate ÷ 100 × years. Total = principal + interest.

Worked example

1,000 at 5% simple interest for 3 years earns 150, giving a total of 1,150.

Assumptions and interpretation

Interest is calculated only on the starting principal. This does not model monthly amortizing loans or changing balances.

Common question

Can I enter part of a year?

Yes. For a six-month estimate enter 0.5 years; actual contracts may use a specific day-count basis.
