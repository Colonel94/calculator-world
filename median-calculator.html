Median Calculator

Find the middle value and range of a numeric list.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter numbers (comma or space separated).
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Sort values. For an odd count, take the middle value; for an even count, average the two middle values.

Worked example

For 1, 2 and 100, the median is 2. For 1, 2, 3 and 100 it is 2.5.

Assumptions and interpretation

The median is less affected by extreme values than the mean. Enter raw observations, not frequency counts.

Common question

Does the input order matter?

No. Values are sorted numerically before the median is selected.
