How to use this calculator

  1. Enter first numerator, first denominator, operation, second numerator, second denominator.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Addition: (ad + bc)/bd. Subtraction: (ad − bc)/bd. Multiplication: ac/bd. Division: ad/bc. Reduce with the greatest common divisor.

Worked example

1/2 + 1/3 = (3 + 2)/6 = 5/6, approximately 0.833333.

Assumptions and interpretation

Denominators must be positive whole numbers. Put a negative sign in the numerator. Inputs are limited to one million to keep integer arithmetic exact.

Common question

Can I divide by a zero fraction?

No. A fraction with numerator zero cannot be used as the divisor.
