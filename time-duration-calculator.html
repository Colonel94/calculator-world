Time Duration Calculator

Calculate time between two clock readings, including an overnight interval.

Example values are filled in. Replace them with your own; all fields are required.

Your result

Check the example values, then select Calculate.

How to use this calculator

  1. Enter start time, end time, end day.
  2. Select Calculate to see the result. After your first calculation, results update as you edit.
  3. Read the assumptions below to check that this model fits your question.

Formula and method

Duration in minutes = end clock minutes − start clock minutes + 1,440 if the end is next day.

Worked example

09:00 to 17:30 on the same day is 8 hours 30 minutes, or 8.5 decimal hours.

Assumptions and interpretation

Select next day explicitly for an overnight interval. No timezone or daylight-saving adjustment is applied; this is a clock-time calculation.

Common question

What happens when start and end are equal?

Same day gives zero; next day gives 24 hours.
